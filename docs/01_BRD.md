# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

**MobCheck — Mobile Phone Marketplace & Professional Inspection Platform**

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** November 27, 2026  
**Status:** In Development

---

## Executive Summary

MobCheck is a Pakistan-based mobile phone marketplace and professional device inspection network. The platform enables customers to discover mobile phones, find verified shops, book professional inspections, and make informed purchasing decisions.

**Initial Market:** Lahore, Pakistan  
**Business Model:** Hybrid marketplace + professional inspection services  
**Primary Revenue:** Inspection fees + shop subscriptions + future commission on sales

---

## Business Problem & Opportunity

### Problem Statement

The used mobile phone market in Pakistan faces significant trust issues:

1. **Information Asymmetry:** Buyers lack reliable information about device condition, history, and authenticity
2. **Fraud Risk:** Fake devices, blacklisted phones, non-PTA approved devices are common
3. **No Standard Inspection:** No standardized professional device inspection service exists
4. **Limited Trust Signals:** No transparent shop verification or buyer reviews
5. **Scattered Market:** Small shops without unified visibility or reputation

### Market Opportunity

- **Large market:** Millions of used phone transactions annually in Pakistan
- **Growing smartphone penetration:** Increasing demand for affordable devices
- **PTA regulations:** Government focus on device authenticity creates compliance need
- **Shop consolidation:** Opportunity to unify scattered mobile shops online
- **Professional gap:** No existing professional inspection service with transparent reports

---

## Business Objectives

1. **Build Trust:** Establish MobCheck as the trusted platform for used phone transactions
2. **Professionalize Market:** Create standardized inspection and verification processes
3. **Empower Buyers:** Give customers confidence to purchase used devices

5. **Generate Revenue:** Create sustainable business through inspections and subscriptions

## Business Model

### Three Sides of the Platform

#### 1. Customers & Individual Sellers
- **Customers:** Browse phones, book inspections, receive verification
- **Individual Sellers:** Create free listings, sell through platform
- **Value Proposition:** Trust, transparency, professional inspection, verified sellers

#### 2. Mobile Shops & Service Providers
- **Shops:** Register business, offer services, receive inspection bookings
- **Inspectors:** Conduct device inspections, provide evidence
- **Value Proposition:** Customer visibility, inspection bookings, reputation building

#### 3. MobCheck Operations
- **Admin Team:** Vendor verification, compliance, payout management
- **Support Team:** Customer service, dispute resolution
- **Operations Team:** Performance monitoring, platform optimization

### Revenue Streams

#### Primary (MVP Launch)

1. **Inspection Fees**
   - Initial price: **Rs. 1,000 per device**
   - Customer pays MobCheck upfront
   - Shop receives payout after 3-hour review window
   - Configurable through admin dashboard

2. **Shop Subscriptions**
   - Free first 30 days
   - Then: **Rs. 1,200/month** (configurable)
   - Includes: Profile listing, booking management, analytics
   - Renewal: Monthly, with 7-day expiry notification

3. **Commission (Future)**
   - Potential commission on private sales through platform
   - To be introduced post-MVP after validation

#### Secondary (Future Phases)

- Premium shop features (analytics, priority support)
- Featured listings
- API access for multi-channel sellers
- Mobile app development
- Geographic expansion to other cities

### Pricing Strategy

**Customer-Facing:**
- Inspection: Rs. 1,000 per device (transparent)
- Listings: Free for individuals
- No hidden fees

**Vendor-Facing:**
- Subscription: Rs. 1,200/month (after trial)
- Takes effect after 30-day free trial
- Clear advance notification before auto-renewal
- Monthly invoice provided

**Configuration:**
- Inspection fee configurable by admin
- Subscription price configurable by admin
- Commission percentage configurable by admin
- All changes audited with effective date

- **Pain Points:** Irregular work, no formal platform for booking
- **Needs:** Consistent work, structured workflow, payment security

## Key Business Rules

### Pricing Rules

| Item | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Inspection Fee | Rs. 1,000 | Configurable, payable upfront by customer |
| Shop Subscription | Rs. 1,200/month | After 30-day free trial |
| Free Trial | 30 days | From registration date |
| Individual Listings | Free | No charge for customers to list phones |
| Commission | TBD | Reserved for future phases |

### Subscription Lifecycle

1. **Days 1-30:** Free trial, full feature access
2. **Day 30:** Auto-renewal notification sent
3. **Day 31:** First payment due
4. **Monthly:** Recurring on same date
5. **Non-payment:** Features restricted (but profile preserved)

### Inspection Workflow

1. Customer or shop initiates booking
2. Payment captured from customer
3. Inspector assigned and confirmed
4. Inspection conducted with checklist
5. Photos and evidence collected
6. Report generated and emailed
7. **3-hour review window begins** ← Critical financial rule
8. After window (or confirmed satisfaction): Payout eligible
9. Partner receives payout (minus fees)

### Financial Settlements

- **Customer Inspection Fee:** Rs. 1,000
- **Payment Gateway Fee:** Variable (to be determined)
- **MobCheck Service Margin:** To be configured
- **Shop Payout:** Remaining amount
- **Timing:** After 3-hour window, net of disputes

## Risks & Mitigation

### High-Risk Items

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|-----------|
| Payment gateway unavailability in Pakistan | Critical | Medium | Research multiple providers, build fallback |
| PTA verification API inaccessibility | High | Medium | Use manual verification, document workflow |
| Shop adoption failure | High | Medium | Strong vendor onboarding, clear value prop |
| Quality inspection issues | High | Medium | Detailed checklists, inspector training, QA |
| Fraud/fake inspections | Critical | Medium | Photo evidence, QR verification, auditing |
| Payment disputes | High | Medium | Clear policies, 3-hour review window, support |

### Regulatory Risks

- **PTA Compliance:** Research PTA registration and device verification requirements
- **Data Protection:** Pakistani data protection and privacy requirements
- **Financial Compliance:** Payment aggregation regulations in Pakistan
- **Consumer Protection:** Pakistani consumer protection laws
